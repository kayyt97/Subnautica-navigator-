Defence & escape

Most dangerous creatures are best avoided, distracted or escaped from.

Seamoth Perimeter Defence

Use a short electric shock to make an attacking creature release your Seamoth. A stronger charge uses more power.

Stasis Rifle

Temporarily freezes many creatures in place. Use the pause to get away rather than staying to fight.

Cyclops safety

Decoys can distract attackers. Silent running and reduced engine noise can help, but drain power faster.

Crabsquid

Its electrical pulse can disable nearby electronics. Turn off vehicle lights and move away if it approaches.

Warper

Can teleport you out of a vehicle. Break line of sight, move away and avoid lingering in its patrol area.

Remember

Getting away safely is a win. You do not need to defeat every creature.

Equipment guide

Useful tools and upgrades for exploring safely.

Scanner

Scan fragments, creatures and technology to unlock blueprints.

Repair Tool

Repairs damaged vehicles, doors and equipment.

Laser Cutter

Opens sealed doors in some wrecks. Bring spare batteries and check oxygen.

Radiation Suit

Protects you around the Aurora while its radiation hazard is active.

Seamoth depth upgrades

Allow the Seamoth to travel deeper without being crushed. Check the module tier before descending.

Oxygen tank

A higher-capacity tank gives more time underwater. Refill it before exploring caves.
